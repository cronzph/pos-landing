⚡ Architecture

How It Works

A hybrid offline-first architecture that gives you the speed of local storage with the reliability of cloud backup.

1
Every Action Hits SQLite First

All reads and writes go directly to your local SQLite database. This makes every operation instant — no waiting for network responses.

2
Background Sync to Firebase

When you're online, changes are automatically pushed to Firebase Firestore in the background. You never wait for it — it just happens.

3
New Device? Auto-Pull Data

Log in on a new device and the app detects an empty local database. It automatically pulls all your data from Firebase — products, transactions, settings, users.

4
Works Offline, Always

Lost internet? No problem. The app continues working 100% normally using local SQLite. When you reconnect, pending changes sync automatically.
